02 Project record · 2026—ongoing

Project
Footprint.

Three milestones in building a private quant research system and a public paper-trading observatory.

Selected milestones

From data
to decisions.

  1. 01

    Build a reliable data foundation.

    What began with market charts grew into historical data collection and storage. Missing sessions and inconsistent feeds made data quality the first priority.

  2. 02

    Develop and test models privately.

    A private backtester supports model search, chronological validation, trading costs and risk limits. Research results guide iteration; they do not establish live profitability.

  3. 03

    Observe the model in live markets.

    Live Lab adds IBKR market-data support and a persistent virtual portfolio. Historical replay is labelled separately from observed paper trading, while model internals remain private. Operational and strategy validation continue.

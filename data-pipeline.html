System design

A short-lived process.
A continuous record.

A scheduler wakes the collector once per minute. It validates the newest completed bar, writes it idempotently, and exits. The page reads only the public view; broker credentials never enter the browser.

  1. 01
    Schedule

    Wake once per market minute.

  2. 02
    Collect

    Request the latest KRX minute bars.

  3. 03
    Validate

    Reject duplicates and incomplete candles.

  4. 04
    Store

    Preserve candles, signals, and paper trades.

  5. 05
    Display

    Append new state without rebuilding the site.
